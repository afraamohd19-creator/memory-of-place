Before the towers

Long before glass and steel, UAE settlements grew around a creek, a fort or an oasis. The people who built them had no air-conditioning and little water, so every lane, wall and wind tower was a careful answer to heat, wind and scarcity. These five places show how UAE cities began, and why keeping them matters for the identity of the country today.

What the five places teach

Four lessons for UAE cities today

Build with the climate

Courtyards, thick walls, wind towers and narrow lanes cooled whole neighbourhoods without machines.

Water decides form

The creek and the falaj chose where people settled, traded and farmed.

Keep the city compact

Short, shaded walks between homes, souqs and mosques made the old towns walkable and social.

Heritage must be lived in

Restored places stay alive when people use them for culture, crafts and daily life.
